About Chronicle

Chronicle is a demo news aggregator built to showcase Adobe Target personalization, Adobe Analytics with A4T reporting, and Customer Attributes for authenticated reader scenarios.

Articles are aggregated every six hours from BBC, TechCrunch, The Verge, and Hacker News. Full text links back to the original publisher — Chronicle does not claim ownership of any content.

The Reader Picker (bottom-right) simulates an authenticated session by calling Visitor.setCustomerIDs with a fake crm_id. This is a demo tool only.
